Boys and Girls

Byline
Michael Elliott
Original publication date
June 19, 2000
Rating
2 stars
Running time
1 hr: 34 min
Source
Original Author Copy

Scripture references

Matthew 5:37Ephesians 5:31Romans 12:9

Translation: KJV
Sermon / spiritual topic: Communication; relationships; honesty; marriage

Author-verified transcription

The king of teenage romantic comedies is on the big screen once again. Freddie Prinze Jr. (Down To You) stars in Boys and Girls, a film that delivers everything in its title... and little else.

In this rendition of a very familiar formula, Mr. Prinze plays Ryan, a geeky kind of guy with thick glasses, a nerdy haircut and a semi-puritan view of sex. His counterpart is Claire Forlani (Meet Joe Black) who plays Jennifer and couldn't be more opposite. Attractive, stylish, and sexually active (just not with Ryan).

These two opposites continually cross each others paths, frustrating and irritating each other, until finally forming a close platonic friendship while attending the same college. Now best friends, they commiserate with one another, cheer each other up, and help each other out. They do everything that a dating couple would do except... that one thing.

Sex. A little word with big implications. Boys and Girls is about sex and what it does to a relationship. Like the proverbial elephant in a china shop no one will talk about, the subject hangs thickly in the air. But the film doesn't give the topic its due. Sanitized and simplified, the movie is content to use sex as the hook to draw in the teenage audiences while keeping well within a formulaic framework and having nothing new or enlightening to say.

Freddie Prinze Jr. offers up his teen idol charm in a role which is hardly a stretch of anyone's talents. Ryan is a typical good, wholesome guy whose hunkiness hides behind his Poindexter glasses and an ill-chosen wardrobe. Not to worry, Ryan eventually escapes the ugly duckling phase and becomes quite a swan.

Claire Forlani, having the more interesting role of Jennifer, makes the most of her screen time. Her fun and carefree persona lightens every scene she is in. It is her performance which drives the movie to its final destination. The other actors merely come along for the ride.

Those others include Jason Biggs (American Pie) who plays Ryan's sex-starved roommate, Amanda Detmer (Final Destination) who plays Jennifer's sexually confused roommate, and Heather Donahue (Blair Witch Project) in a role which is little more than a cameo but which allows us to see her out of the woods and freshly showered.

All (or almost all) of the sexuality is implied rather than displayed. There is no nudity and the sexual coupling is kept off screen. There is a brief lesbian encounter which is surprising as it suddenly appears out of nowhere and goes back there just as quickly.

For all its titillation, sex is not the central obstacle affecting the central characters in this film. The problem they have is not a physical one but a communicative one. For the majority of the film, nobody says what they are thinking or feeling. They instead say what they think the other person wants to hear. That is no way to build a true loving relationship.

Communication is a vital part of any relationship. Without it, there is no trust... There is no believing together... There is no sharing.

Simply let your 'Yes' be 'Yes,' and your 'No,' 'No'; anything beyond this comes from the evil one. Matthew 5:37 (KJV)

The sign of a mature relationship is not one that is marked by a physical consummation. Rather, it is the one that is marked by a commitment and full sharing communication between the partners. Animals can have sex. There is nothing unique or special in two creatures merely engaging in a physical union. The wonder of a true godly relationship is found in the like-minded fellowship of a man and a woman who share all with each other, growing together as if they were one body. With that as the core of the relationship, such as marriage should be, the physical union that can grow from honest communication is indeed something beautiful and wonderful.
