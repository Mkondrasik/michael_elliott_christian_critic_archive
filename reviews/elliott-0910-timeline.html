Author-verified transcription

Timeline was a rather enjoyable novel by the reliably entertaining Michael Crichton. It has now become an almost intolerable action/adventure movie under the hand of director/producer Richard Donner.

When archaeology professor Edward Johnston, (Billy Connolly), disappears during one of his digs, his students find clues among the ruins that indicate he has somehow been transported back to the 14th century. Upon further investigation they find that the financial backers of their expedition have accidentally uncovered a wormhole in time into which the professor entered.

The professor's son (Paul Walker) and the other students agree to follow him into the wormhole in order to rescue Johnston and bring him back to the present day.

They arrive near the French village of Castlegard in 1357 only to find themselves smack in the middle of The Hundred Years War on the eve of a pivotal (and viciously brutal) battle. Having only a few hours before their return becomes impossible, they must somehow evade the combatants, find the professor, and reconnect with the wormhole... Oh yes, all the while taking care as to not upset the fabric of time by altering the past. Nah... Might as well forget that last point, it's probably not important.

With Paul Walker, his California good looks and his severely limited acting abilities heading the cast, Timeline might be better subtitled Gidget Goes Feudal. All we need do is substitute swords for surfboards.

Surprisingly, there seems to have been little attempt to elevate the film to the level of its source material. Logic, character development, and a smoothly flowing narrative are elements which must have been deemed superfluous by the filmmakers because they've left them out of the final product.

What's left are a few 21st century young people engaging in histrionics as flaming arrows whiz through the air and trebuchets launch flaming boulders into fortress walls.

The only cast member who seems to have a proper grasp of the material is Gerard Butler, but it isn't until his character is separated from the others that he is given the opportunity to show us. When grouped together, the entire cast puts on an embarrassing display of poor improvisational skills.

Richard Donner will be remembered for directing such winners as Superman and the Lethal Weapon series. It is best for him that we let Timeline fade into a forgotten history.

A film where time travel is a predominant element can serve to remind us that historical records are more than dry and lifeless stories. These records represent a time and a place where real people had to make real decisions and face the real consequences of those decisions.

The biblical records which make up the Scriptures were indeed written for our learning but the people who lived those tales were as real as you and I. It sometimes helps to remember that as we study our Bibles so that the lessons we're learning will gain a deeper meaning as we recognize how they pertain to life and living.

For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope. Romans 15:4 (KJV)
