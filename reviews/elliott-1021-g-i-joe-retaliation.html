G.I. Joe: Retaliation

Author-verified transcription

As any child of the 60’s knows, a G.I. Joe action figure never actually did anything on its own. The same can be said of this newest film that bears its name. I suppose we adults should always be wary of any action film that includes in its opening credits, “In Association with Hasbro.”

What “plot” there is in G.I. Joe: Retaliation seems to revolve around some nuclear weapons, a phony president, more artillery than most countries have in their national armory, and a few “Joes” left alive after a traitorous attack decimated most of their fighting force.

The muscular Dwayne Johnson is left to lead the few brave souls who are willing to put their lives on the line for the sake of something they feel is worth dying for. They don’t really explain it to the rest of us. We can assume it to be freedom, love of country, truth, justice or the American Way… but this film doesn’t spell it out. Nor does it really seem to matter.

There are explosions galore, martial arts fighting in just about every battle, and characters that are so bland and interchangeable you’ll be forgiven if you forget their names even before the movie’s end.

There has been a lot written of late regarding Hollywood’s tailoring their product to appeal to the international marketplace and perhaps that is why G.I. Joe’s natural American persona in this film seems to have been rachetted down to a more generic level. There isn’t a lot of flag waving or national pride to speak of in this film and to my mind it is a detriment to the overall impact or enjoyment to be received.

G.I. Joe should represent the best and brightest among us; they are supposed to be soldiers who not only believe in the ideals upon which this country was founded but are also willing to defend those who continue to live by those ideals. Woe to the country who loses its ability to instill the kind of patriotism in their people that would lead able men to rise to the defense and protection of its way of life.

“And I sought for a man among them, that should make up the hedge, and stand in the gap before me for the land, that I should not destroy it: but I found none.” Ezekiel 22:30 (KJV)
