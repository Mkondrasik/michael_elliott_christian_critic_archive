Author-verified transcription

It really isn’t fair to call the featured artists of 20 Feet From Stardom “unsung” heroes, because they can all sing their hearts out and have on many of the popular hits we know and fondly remember from our youth. Backup singers Darlene Love, Merry Clayton, Lisa Fischer, Tata Vega, and Judith Hill among others are brought front and center in an award-winning documentary that finally puts a well-deserved spotlight on these exceedingly talented vocalists.

As stated by many of the artists who appear on screen (Bruce Springsteen, Sting, Bette Midler, and Mick Jagger, etc.) the importance of the “wall of sound” that supports and enhances the featured solo performer can never be overestimated. Darlene Love and The Blossoms started a revolution that literally changed the music industry. Hearing their raw and emotional fervor honed and crafted from the gospel choirs in which they were raised, solo artists sought to add that sound to their own works and the only way they could accomplish it is to hire them (and others who followed in their footsteps) to bring it with them into the studio.

Though instrumental in the success of mega-hit after mega-hit, the ladies received little credit, no glory, and, in some cases, were cheated by industry power brokers who exploited their talents but kept them from achieving the solo successes that were rightfully theirs. They were all well known in the industry but remained relatively anonymous to the record-buying public. That is, until now. This film is a great tribute to these dedicated professionals who clearly have a love of music.

It should come as no surprise that these ladies have a common origin story. The music of praise, expressing thanks, awe and wonder towards our heavenly Father strikes a chord within us that transcends understanding. These singers tapped into that level of emotion and learned to incorporate it to any song they were given to sing. God gave them a gift to use, and, by using it, were able to give Him the glory.

When Darlene Love stepped onto the Academy Awards stage to accept the Oscar for Best Documentary, she sang her acceptance speech by belting out the gospel hymn “His Eye Is On The Sparrow.” It was a riveting, emotional moment shared by millions around the globe as she demonstrated her faith.

Regardless of our backgrounds, experiences, or ambitions, God has given each of us talents or gifts to use in this world. As we use them we should be reminded that all we have comes from God and He does watch over us. We may never span that 20 Feet that separates us from Stardom, but there is no gulf that divides us from God. We are stars in His eyes. He is our biggest fan. And, when all is said and done, that should be enough for us.
