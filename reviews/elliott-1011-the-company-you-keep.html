The Company You Keep

Byline
Michael Elliott
Original publication date
August 5, 2013
Rating
2 stars
Running time
2 hrs: 1 min
Source
Original Author Copy

Scripture references

Luke 12:2Isaiah 32:17Ecclesiastes 12:14

Sermon / spiritual topic: Truth; Honesty; Consequences; Righteousness

Author-verified transcription

God tells us that all things hidden will one day come to light. For Nick Sloan, those aren’t exactly the words he wants to hear.

Nick (Robert Redford) was a member of a radical faction of an anti-war protest group who called themselves The Weathermen. In the late 70s, they staged a bank robbery in which a guard was killed. Placed on the FBI’s most wanted list, they’ve been in hiding ever since.

As we jump forward 30 years, Nick and his fellow Weathermen have gone their own ways, assumed new identities and built lives for themselves. All of which threatens to come unraveled when one of them decides to stop hiding and give herself up to the authorities. A local NY reporter (Shia LeBoeuf) locks onto the story and begins to dig for the truth… uncomfortably close to people who would rather have it remain unrevealed.

The film begins with a lot of promise – a great cast and director, interesting storyline and premise, more than a hint of mystery, and intelligent characters that we can care about. It is a pity that the filmmakers could not figure out how to sustain the interest level throughout its two-hour running time. The film unfortunately ends with a whimper instead of a bang.

“Oh what a tangled web we weave when first we practice to deceive,” Sir Walter Scott once observed. Thirty years is a long time to live a lie. And during that time, the Weathermen drew others into their lies and involved them (knowingly or unknowingly) in their deceptions. So much trouble and bother and all they did was delay the inevitable.

“For there is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed; neither hid, that shall not be known.” Luke 12:2 (KJV)

The Weathermen, though committed to a cause they believed was just, made mistakes that cost an innocent man his life. And instead of showing repentance or atoning for the pain they caused, they chose to avoid the consequences of their action by creating new identities and disguising the truth. Anyone living a lie (knowingly or unknowingly) becomes a slave to that lie. The weight of a lie grows on a soul the longer one tries to carry it. There is a rare freedom that comes from living honestly. It is a peace that God wants us to know and experience.

“The fruit of that righteousness will be peace; its effect will be quietness and confidence forever.” Isaiah 32:17 (NIV)
