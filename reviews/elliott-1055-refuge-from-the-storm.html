Refuge From the Storm

Sermon / spiritual topic: Spiritual Warfare; Deliverance; Evil; God’s Protection

Author-verified transcription

Utilizing an unusual distribution approach, the independent film Refuge From the Storm is creeping its way across the nation by way of privately hosted screening events. Unabashed in its Christian message, the film tells the reportedly true story of a young woman drawn into a spiritual battle and how she is delivered from the ensuing darkness.

Linda (Kristen Quintrall), is an emotionally troubled female with a childhood history of abuse, subsequent bouts of depression and severe migraine attacks. Her life takes a turn for the better after a chance meeting leads her to a Christian retreat where the Word of God is revealed to her. There she learns of God’s deliverance, goodness and love. Meanwhile, Steve (Michael Madsen) is a writer who has chosen to tackle the ominous subject of the struggle between good and evil. Unfortunately, he decides to research the evil side of things first.

Both become aware of the invisible reality of the spiritual forces vying for their souls. For these characters, the fight between good and evil is not philosophical or theoretical. It is real and it has real consequences.

Quintrall is very believable in a difficult role. Much of the film has her in a state of high anxiety, fear or desperation but she finds the levels and the character arc needed to take us on the journey with her. Madsen should be commended for lending his screen presence to what is obviously a low-budget Christian film. While he may arguably be miscast in the role of Steve, his name on the marquee does lend a touch of credibility to the enterprise. In a lesser role, Angie Jerez is fascinating to watch as she portrays a woman possessed and delivered from a spiritual demon. It is an impressive portrayal.

One’s level of appreciation for Refuge From the Storm may indeed be determined by one’s degree of Christian faith. For those who study the Bible and believe the biblical records that deal with devil spirits and the healing power of God may be enthralled with the fact that there’s a feature film that lines up with their beliefs and teachings. Those who scoff at the scriptures may feel compelled to do the same with this film lest they be forced to reconsider their spiritual stance.

Evidence of the invisible spiritual war that exists between good and evil appears throughout the scriptures. Jesus Christ, during his earthly ministry, exposed the devil and explained many of his methods and means. We are warned of the consequences of wandering into the darkness and exhorted to the benefits of staying in God’s wondrous light. We have been granted the power to repel the devilish forces that attempt to influence our choices and deflect our worship from God.

“For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.” Ephesians 6:12 (KJV)

We may not be able to prevent the spiritual “storms” from raging in this world. But we can be assured that God does provide for us a refuge from them where we can be protected.
