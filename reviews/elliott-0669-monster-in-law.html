Author-verified transcription

No one will be mistaking the humor of Monster-in-Law as being that of a sophisticated wit. It's as subtle as a slap in the face... an event which occurs repeatedly in the film.

Jennifer Lopez stars as Charlotte or Charley as she is called by her friends. She's an attractive temp worker who divides her time between walking dogs, answering phones for a doctor, carrying trays of shrimp balls for a caterer and waiting for Mr. Right.

When she meets and falls in love with Dr. Kevin Fields (Michael Vartan), their romance grows at a quick and unhindered pace... largely because the one obstacle to her happiness has been locked up in a sanitarium.

The obstacle is Kevin's mother. Viola (Jane Fonda). She's a famous news anchor who has just been terminated by her network in favor of a younger model. She's also an overbearing diva who can't stand the idea of her precious son marrying beneath him. Unfortunately for Charley, Viola is released from the funny farm just in time to upset the wedding plans. She plans to drive Charley from Kevin's arms by being totally obnoxious and unbearable.

The film, directed by Robert Luketic (Legally Blonde), relies too heavily upon cheap jokes, crude humor and the appeal of its stars. Only the latter gets anything close to a passing grade. The script by Anya Kochoff is barely passable - a formulaic, wafer-thin story that is as implausible as it is predictable. It is good only to serve as a platform upon which actors may exercise their comedic skills. Too few rise to the challenge.

Jennifer Lopez keeps the same persona she used in her other romantic comedies (Maid in Manhattan, The Wedding Planner). She's cute, perky and inoffensively bland. All in all, she's a perfect match for the cute, perky and inoffensively bland Michael Vartan.

The only life or sassiness of the film comes from Wanda Sykes who, as personal assistant to Viola, makes the most of her limited screen time. She's given the best lines and underplays them with a wry but earthy awareness. Adam Scott also adds a few nice touches as one of Charley's oddball friends.

It's been fifteen years since Academy Award winning actress Jane Fonda last graced the screen and the time off has had its effect on her craft. She never fully inhabits the character or takes the risks needed to make Viola a real comedic showcase. Instead she offers up a string of stereotypical characteristics with nothing of substance behind them. As a result, we don't care about Viola or the situation in which she finds herself.

Viola's plan is simple enough - drive the bride-to-be crazy by making her think that she would be the mother-in-law from hell. As ludicrous a plan it may be - it could easily work.

A continual dripping on a day of violent showers and a contentious woman are alike. Proverbs 27:15 (Amplified)

Just as a continual dripping can cause the erosion of even the strongest of rocks, a continual barrage from a contentious woman will have a negative effect upon even the strongest of souls.

For this reason, God exhorts us to choose our words carefully and to speak to one another in love. When we do, we form the bonds that serve to strengthen our relationships and bless our lives.
