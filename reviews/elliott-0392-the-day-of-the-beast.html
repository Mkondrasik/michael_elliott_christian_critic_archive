Author-verified transcription

Trimark Home Video is preparing to release the 1995 Spanish film, The Day of the Beast in video format. Winner of six Goya awards (the Spanish equivalent of the Oscars), this darkly comedic horror film tells a confusing tale of a priest hell-bent to destroy the Antichrist... if he only knew where to find him.

Directed and co-written by Alex de la Iglesia (Accion Mutante) with his tongue perversely planted in cheek, the film is short on logic, but long on violence and shock humor. Indiscriminate murders, muggings, and satanic rituals are in abundant supply.

Alex Angulo, who also appeared in Accion Mutante, stars as Padre Angel who has just discovered the key to the Apocalypse, numerically buried in the scriptures. It indicates that the Antichrist will be born in a scant 24 hours and our good Padre has decided it has fallen upon him to kill the evil babe in order to save all of mankind.

To succeed in his quest, Angel enlists the aid of Professor Cavan (Armando De Razza, Il Grande cocomero), a scheming and cheesy television "prophet" and Jose Maria (Santiago Segura, Accion Mutante) a satanic record store clerk with an obnoxious mother and a grandfather who has an unexplained tendency to go "sans pants" (i.e. butt naked).

Much of the movie involves Angel's attempts to invoke Satan, presumably to convince him to tell them where to find the baby Antichrist. Along the way there is bloodshed, mayhem, atrocities committed against innocent people, and enough sin to break God's heart.

Alex Angulo (a Spanish Stanley Tucci) has an earnestness and a deadpan expression that is initially amusing as the film sets its premise. But as the story progresses, and his character immerses himself into a world of darkness and evil, the comic elements of the film begin to wane.

Angel has the idea that he has to turn evil in order to be accepted by Satan and gain the knowledge needed to thwart his plans. Perhaps seeing a priest commit heinous acts is good for a laugh or two in Spain. It did not translate well to this reviewer.

Of course, the movie's premise is ridiculously erroneous. Scriptures tell us of the antichrist... Many of them, in fact, that are alive and active in our world today.

For many deceivers are entered into the world, who confess not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh. This is a deceiver and an antichrist. 2 John 1:7

There are many antichrists in the world today. Just as they have been in existence throughout time. The truth is that we can stand against them, and thwart their intentions... Not by adopting their ways but by resisting evil and standing for truth.
