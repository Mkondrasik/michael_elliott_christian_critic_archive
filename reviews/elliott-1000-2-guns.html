Author-verified transcription

There are a lot more than 2 guns on display in the movie 2 Guns. Pyrotechnics, explosions, and gunplay rule the day… or at least the 1 ¾ hours of the film’s running time.

Denzel Washington stars as DEA agent Bobby Trench who has been working undercover for the past three years to take down a Mexican drug lord (Edward James Olmos). During his investigation, he has made contact and formed a relationship with Stig Stigman (Mark Wahlberg), who has concocted a plan to rob a bank in order to flush the drug lord into the open.

What Bobby doesn’t realize is that Stigman is also working undercover. As a naval intelligence officer, he has orders to rob the bank and take the money that is there. The money turns out to be a whole lot more than either of them were expecting. It is a sum that tends to get people killed.

Bobby and Stigman are forced to look past their mutual deceptions and work together to unravel the mess in which they find themselves. But can they trust each other?

The mood is light and humorous. The chemistry between Washington and Wahlberg works and is very entertaining. The other players in the film do justice to their respective roles but are quickly forgotten as the film plods on. When all is said and done, this is really a two- character film and it helps that the two characters are themselves played by two very accomplished actors.

For Stigman, who has a defined code of ethics, the situation is simple. We are to fight for the guy fighting beside us. It is not a bad creed to adopt for one’s life. It can be applied to both a physical battle as well as a spiritual competition. As two individuals fight for the same cause, and they choose to fight as one, their unified power is increased exponentially.

“Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour. For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up.” Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 (KJV)

When Jesus sent his disciples into the world to spread the good news of his appearance, he sent them in pairs. The spiritual reality is that a union of like-minded individuals will always accomplish more than a lone wolf.
