Author-verified transcription

Jackass: The Movie opens with a huge disclaimer filling the screen which pretty much warns the viewers "not to try this at home." I don't know what's more disturbing - the fact that this movie was made in the first place, the fact that it will most likely make tons of money, or the fact that there may be people dumb enough to want to emulate the idiotic, self-destructive, and perverse behavior which is passing for a motion picture released by a major studio.

This big screen version of the MTV comedy show is a plotless exercise which pushes the limits of taste and human endurance. The so-called "stunts" amount to little more than self-inflicted abuse. Many of them I won't even attempt to describe. Suffice it to say that no body cavity goes unexplored and all bodily fluids and excretions are at one point filmed. Indeed, the titular characters seem overjoyed when their antics result in someone losing their lunch or loosing their bowels.

When not abusing themselves or each other, they inflict their sociopathic stunts on a supposedly unsuspecting public. I suppose that in a free country, self mutilation or masochism is a matter of personal choice but when did it become permissible to accost people in the street or at their places of work or even in the sanctity of their own homes?

What I don't fully understand is the entertainment value in all this. While I'm as much of a fan of slapstick humor as anyone else, what Jackass does can't really be called slapstick humor. Sick humor perhaps but even there I'd have to question the humor part.

I frankly don't see the entertainment value of watching people debase or demean themselves and others. Without trying to be judgmental, what does it say about us as a people that we can be amused by watching someone purposely hurt himself? Granted, some of the ways they've invented to inflict pain and suffering are unique and often shocking but amusing? Not by any standard of which I'm aware.

The Webster Dictionary defines jackass as being "a stupid or foolish person; a nitwit." At least this film is titled well. Here's what the scriptures have to say about fools:

"Even though like grain you should pound a fool in a mortar with a pestle, yet will not his foolishness depart from him." Proverbs 27:22 (Amplified)

A fool exists by reason of his very actions. The two cannot be separated. If foolishness exists, it is because there is a fool in the midst of it. Or in the case of this movie, several fools.

"It is as sport to a fool to do mischief." Proverbs 10:23a (KJV)

Reasoning with a fool is pointless. Their logic is flawed because it is based upon a warped sense of values.

He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed. Proverbs 13:20 (KJV)

The above scripture offers a word of advice to anyone thinking that the jackass lifestyle is a desirable one. Any success will be short lived. The end result will not be a pretty one.

"As a dog returneth to his vomit, so a fool returneth to his folly." Proverbs 26:11 (KJV)

Like it or not... there's going to be a sequel. Perhaps next time we, as the movie-going public, might spend our box office dollars more wisely. If we keep on encouraging them by engaging in their foolishness, they'll only keep on doing it.
