Author-verified transcription

Billed as a comedy, the award-winning Dutch film Iles Flottantes (translated as Floating Islands) provides very few laughs as its three main female characters simply "float" through life until they finally come face to face with the churning waters that mark the beginning of their 30th year. Suddenly, floating aimlessly is becoming more and more unappealing but after so long, they have no idea how to do anything else.

More like sisters than friends, Kate, Ilsa, and Sasha spend their days in the tanning beds, swimming laps, vacationing in Russia, and generally making poor choices regarding whom they welcome to their beds... something they do with noticeable frequency.

Sasha is the most levelheaded of this trio and she has a wife-beating father with whom she has broken off all contact and a boyfriend who is not only physically abusive to her, he abuses drugs as well. If Sasha is the most levelheaded in the midst of that, one can only imagine the shape of the other two.

Ilsa is a self-doubting, insecure artist with delusions of mediocrity. The only thing she seems confident in is the love she feels toward Christophe, a fellow artist who has been secretly carrying on an affair with Kate, one of Ilsa's closest friends. This is the same Kate who, the other surmise, has the most successful love relationship of the three.

Little do they know.

In fact, that seems to be the point. Little do any of them know, because all three, as close as they are, never seem to talk about anything of consequence.

Friends, true friends, should be able to share fully their hopes, dreams, goals, and fears with one another. Part of being a friend is to help a person realize their dreams, achieve their goals and defeat their fears. Because of the support and encouragement one derives from such a relationship, a friendship can be the one of the most valuable human connections anyone can have.

Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend. Proverb 27:17 (KJV)

If we are to sharpen the countenance of those we call friends, a crucial thing we must do is maintain honest and open communication. If that is lost, so is the friendship.
