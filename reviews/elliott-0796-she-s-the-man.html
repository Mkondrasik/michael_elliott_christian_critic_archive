Author-verified transcription

Shakespeare's Twelfth Night is reworked (or should that be worked over?) in the new teen comedy She's the Man. To say that it is based on the Bard's classic comedy is to say that hot dogs and baked beans are based on a gourmet meal.

The comedy stems from the dilemma that Viola (Amanda Bynes) faces. Her school has cut the girl's soccer program due to lack of funds/interest. Viola, after being turned down by the coach of the boy's team, turns to the only alternative she feels is available. She pretends to be her twin brother, Sebastian (James Kirk), who is off traveling in Europe.

Sebastian/Viola enters college and immediately signs up for the school's soccer program but to keep the charade going, she/he must appear to be a guy. Of course he/she has a male roommate, Duke (Channing Tatum) who becomes her/his romantic interest. But Duke is more interested in Olivia (Laura Ramsey) who, ironically enough, has become smitten with Sebastian (who is Viola in disguise).

Meanwhile Sebastian has a girlfriend, Monica (Alex Breckenridge) who he no longer wants but she pursues him anyway (or at least she pursues who she thinks to be him but is now, in actuality, Viola in disguise). Get it?

The crux of the comedy is that Sebastian and Viola are twins and, when dressed alike, they are indistinguishable one from the other. The comedy is greatly decreased when the illusion doesn't work. And with Amanda Bynes as Viola/Sebastian, it doesn't. We just don't buy her as a guy. It's one thing to wink at the audience and ask them to suspend their disbelief. It's quite another to insult their intelligence and ask them to spend their hard earned money on such uninspired drivel.

Amanda Bynes, an actress for whom I had such promise after seeing her in Big Fat Liar, has resorted to mugging instead of acting. Her character isn't grounded or solidly based. She expects the premise to sell the film without getting us to buy the premise.

Her co-stars don't have the same dilemma because they don't carry the same responsibility. Channing Tatum presents himself as a romantic heartthrob though he does nothing to distinguish himself from the tens of thousands of other good looking Hollywood actors looking for a break. Vinnie Jones, as a no-nonsense soccer coach, is serviceable... as is Julie Haggerty as Viola's mom, James Kirk as Viola's twin, and Laura Ramsey as the lynchpin to the romantic quagmire.

Of course, Viola's own duplicity is the major obstacle standing in the way of her finding true love. When we set out to deceive, we will often find it is our deception that is what prevents us from attaining our goals.

(Pro 20:17) Bread of deceit is sweet to a man; but afterwards his mouth shall be filled with gravel. Proverbs 20:17 (KJV)

The best love to have is not something that is won. It is a love that is freely given. It is not a love that we have to fight for or earn. It is one that flows naturally and grows with time. It knows no deception and requires no ruse. It is, simply put, the best part of us.

And now faith, hope, and love, these three things remain; but the greatest of these is love. 1 Corinthians 13:13 (LIV)
