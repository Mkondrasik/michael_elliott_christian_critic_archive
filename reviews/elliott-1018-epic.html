Author-verified transcription

The ongoing battle between life and death or growth and decay is the central theme to the animated fantasy adventure aptly named Epic.

After the death of her mother, teenaged MK (Amanda Seyfried) is sent to the home of her estranged father (Jason Sudeikis), an odd scientist with an even odder belief: that the forest contains a micro-world of little people who control the ecosystem. Oddest of all, MK discovers that he is right when she is magically transported into this tiny world where Leafmen protect the forest and its denizens from the decaying blight of the Boggins.

The Queen of the Forest (Beyonce Knowles) has chosen her successor. The identity of the new queen is contained in a pod that must bloom in full moonlight or else the forest will die. Mandrake (Christolph Waltz), has marshaled his dark Boggin forces to prevent the pod from providing the forest with a new queen. All that stands in his way are the Leafman, led by Ronin (Colin Farrell), MK, and a brash loner named Nod (Josh Hutcherson). In the balance is life itself – for them and for the world in which they live.

Instead of using the film as a platform for environmental reforms or warnings (which might have been a natural expectation), director Chris Wedge simply tells an adventure story that may appeal to the young or young at heart. There’s no real moralizing going on – just plenty of action. The detail of the computer animation is also first rate.

The power of the Queen to regenerate and spur growth is the key to keeping the decay from engulfing the forest. There is a spiritual analogy that can be drawn from the natural balance we see in this world.

“And the Light shines on in the darkness, for the darkness has never overpowered it [put it out or absorbed it or appropriated it, and is unreceptive to it].” John 1:5 (Amplified)

Just as the rot of the Boggins could not spread across the forest while the Queen’s power was present, the light of God’s Word holds the darkness of our spiritual adversary at bay. Light always triumphs over darkness. They cannot co-exist.

As long as there are men and women who continue to believe and speak the truths of God, shining as lights in this world, the spiritual darkness that exists cannot overtake or overwhelm us. It is only when we choose to stop walking in the light that we become susceptible to its influence.
