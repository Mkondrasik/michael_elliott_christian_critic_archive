ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

Michael Elliott / Christian Critic Archive

Michael Elliott was the byline used by author Michael Ondrasik for film reviews published through The Christian Critic and Movie Parables. The archive is being reconstructed from surviving author copies and other primary materials.

The project has two complementary purposes: to preserve the reviews as historical documents and to make their biblical references useful to readers, pastors, teachers, and researchers.

Editorial and preservation principles

The transcription preserves the author’s wording, punctuation, capitalization, opinions, and translation labels. OCR errors are corrected; prose is not silently rewritten. “Author Verified” means Michael Ondrasik has reviewed the searchable transcription.

Dates printed on some surviving working copies are not treated as publication dates because some were inherited from reused templates. Only an independently established or author-established original publication date is displayed.

Original working copies may contain later handwritten annotations. Those annotations are preserved in the private archival source but are excluded from the public-facing scan. Internet Archive links will point only to approved public archival files.
